About

Nuclear energy offers a high-density, low-carbon source of electricity. Compared to fossil fuels, reactors release virtually no greenhouse gases and operate independently of weather conditions, providing steady baseload power.

Benefits of Nuclear Energy

  • Produces large amounts of electricity with minimal greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Requires far less land than wind or solar installations for equivalent output.
  • Provides consistent, reliable power unlike intermittent renewable sources.

Safety Advancements

  • Generation III+ reactors use passive safety systems that cool and shut down reactors without human intervention.
  • Small modular reactors are factory-built, incorporate redundant safety features, and reduce on-site construction risk.
  • Modern fuel designs and improved waste management enhance containment and storage safety.
